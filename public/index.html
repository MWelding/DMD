FROM THE SUPPLIED SUPPORTING STUDIES

Change in Progress explores the public transport of Riga through memory, contrast, and movement. Monochrome vehicles carry the weight of routine. Colour reveals the energy of change. The station holds both worlds together.

The collage develops the supplied research into Constructivist geometry, photographic fragments, surreal scale, and a moving spotlight. The dates 1975, 2000, and 2025 are interpretive chapters, rather than dates attributed to the reference photographs.

HOW TO EXPLORE

Move your pointer to reveal colour. Select an object or use Tab and Enter to open its story. Choose an era below the artwork. On a touchscreen, drag across the collage to move the lens and tap a label to open a story.

Developed from the supplied “Change in Progress” supporting studies for the transport digital-installation brief. Photographs remain the property of their respective creators. Source and location notes appear inside each story. Sound is generated locally and starts only when enabled.
